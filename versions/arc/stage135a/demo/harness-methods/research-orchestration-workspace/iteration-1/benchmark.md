| Configuration | Assertions passed | Pass rate | Time/tokens |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |

| without skill | 10 / 10 | 100% | unavailable |

Observed pass-rate delta: **+0.00**.

This comparison does not show a skill benefit. The graders found that the prompts
already cue much of the desired policy and several assertions combine multiple
behaviors, so both conditions can pass without exposing finer orchestration
differences. With only one run per configuration, variance is also unknown. Token
and timing data were unavailable and were not estimated.

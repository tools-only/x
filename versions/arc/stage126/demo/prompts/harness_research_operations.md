# Research reference

Operation-specific instructions retained from the pre-optimization prompts.
These apply under their stated conditions; on-demand delivery does not make
required steps optional. Runtime receipts supply current references and state.

Use Auto-Research for a grounded uncertainty or missing solving capability. Before
starting supply known conditions and accessible materials, a bounded useful
deliverable and a cost/stop bound. A question may concern the goal itself, competing
strategies or how to evaluate them. If an environment trial is not yet definable,
research may construct a representation, consistency check or experiment design;
do not require its answer as a launch prerequisite. Keep the overall task purpose
while bounding the investigation, and distinguish proposed checks from executed ones.
Research may compare granted evidence across phases and construct candidate
methods from prior knowledge, then test them locally. Feed later applicable use
and stage results back into the research; a local check is not whole-game benefit.

The review persists the parent's own transition_analysis and returns a
research_handoff_ref plus explicit blocking and non_blocking Auto-Research call
templates. Auto-Research validates or challenges that summary; it does not replace
the 5/20 parent induction. For a worthwhile operation, main owns the choice among
direct creator-based development, an optional grounded research question, or an
operation-specific deferral with its missing prerequisite and reconsideration
condition. Research is not a prerequisite and a pending handoff does not block the
next action. Omit research_handoff_ref for a unique eligible handoff. Multiple
targets return complete reference candidates for selection; never assemble or
guess versioned refs. Defer can be reactivated manually or after a declared number
of successful actions. If runtime reports budget exhaustion or infrastructure
degradation, continue the parent task rather than repeatedly repairing that gate.
Incremental handoffs own the structured `research_problem`: an actionable uncertainty
or capability bottleneck grounded by the current window. A valid result may be a
research conclusion, counterevidence, planning implication, experiment request,
single-situation method, broader candidate method, or an exact maturity/access gap.
Do not force a component-shaped abstraction when the evidence supports only useful
research. A mechanism hypothesis and discriminating environment sample are evidence
for the problem; a bare window recap without a decision-relevant result is incomplete.
Consolidation also examines methods using relevant accessible cross-phase contrasts. Check the
research preparation conditions even for generated handoffs; explicitly defer/skip
duplicate handoffs and carry useful evidence to existing research. Execute ARC
experiments only in the parent, then resume eligible pending/failed sessions with
the new evidence. Completed work needs a bounded follow-up citing its exact prior
research_run/research_report, not resume. Inspect active work rather than duplicating it.
Research agreement or completion never raises confidence without cited evidence.

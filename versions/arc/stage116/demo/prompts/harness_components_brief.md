# Task-local components

Main authors and chooses by the task's missing operation, uncertainty or reusable context; component counts are not a goal. Research is optional. A first-use or local candidate may be untested: state its uncertainty and test what budget and access permit. Admission does not certify meaning, correctness or benefit.

- Memory holds a scoped fact, hypothesis or plan atom. A formal create/change candidate is {component:"memory",key:"stable-name",content:"parent-authored statement",atom:{subject:"...",predicate:"...",value:"..."},validity:{scope:"task|episode|level|state"}}. Choose one actual scope value. Runtime binds omitted task/instance identifiers. Split independently changing atoms; retain the key and current target_version for updates. A map is level scoped; a task-scoped control rule needs its control-mode conditions. Current frame, position and budget already come from runtime. Optional free-form task_memory_pool capture is a separate pool-only path.
- System Prompt guides decisions within an authored scope, including provisional exploration or planning policy. Its candidate is {component:"system_prompt",name:"stable-name",content:"parent-authored policy",scope:"when this applies"}; include a reconsideration condition in the policy. This cannot change platform permissions. Facts do not become directives automatically.
- Skill teaches inputs, ordered steps, decisions, outputs, uncertainty and stopping. Read skill-creator once and fill its returned complete template; a recap is not a procedure.
- Tool executes supported bounded computation with declared inputs, outputs and a complete program. Read tool-creator once; check actual values and a contrast, not just completed status.
- Subagent saves a bounded responsibility and prepared independent context. Read subagent-creator once; inspect the answer, evidence, cost and limitations. Ordinary delegate_task is available for one-off reasoning without a saved role.

Publish complete candidates with task_harness_create or task_harness(change), using decision={basis_refs,reason,expected}. Optional assembly={expected_assembly_revision:N,selected_resource_refs:[existing refs to retain]} explicitly selects this call's written versions plus those existing refs. Omit it for pool-only creation. If selection fails after writing, repair it without recreating written versions. Native receipts give exact versions and use entries; composition persists until changed. Use Skill read without narrowing other selections, invoke Tool or delegate Subagent, apply results to a task decision and inspect feedback. Creation, exposure and agreement alone prove no benefit.

Declare exact depends_on_refs for validity dependencies and supersedes_refs for replacements; basis_refs alone are historical evidence. Keep canonical bodies and projections consistent. Revise, suspend or retire contradicted guidance. Full details: contract(topic=components,section=all).

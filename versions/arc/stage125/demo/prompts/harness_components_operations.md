Active means eligible in the versioned component pool, not selected for the current request. `task_memory_pool(action="capture")` is the parent-facing low-friction path for adding batches of local observations, hypotheses, plans and occasional retrospectives while work is in progress. The pool retains many candidates; capture does not add them to prompt context. Use `task_memory_pool(action="list")` for a bounded directory, `task_resource(action="read", ref=...)` for exact full contents, and `task_harness(action="assemble")` to select exact eligible versions. Direct environment action does not require reassembly.

When the goal lacks an operation, or the next step requires re-deriving a mapping,
plan or check already used, decide whether making that operation reusable will
improve later progress, reliability or cost. First-use development is valid: choose
the smallest fitting carrier, read its creator if required, author with
task_harness(action=change), assemble the exact version, then use and assess it.
Direct action remains a valid choice; preserve unfinished work only when you have
chosen to develop an operation and explicitly defer it. Review/followup boundaries expose a development-reference call; read
task_harness(action="contract", topic="development") for the full process.

Research is optional.
Choose by function, not presumed permanence. A system_prompt segment can carry a
provisional task-, level- or phase-scoped working policy: how to explore, plan,
compare strategies or revisit assumptions across the next relevant decisions.
Author its scope and reconsideration condition, then explicitly assemble it;
revise/replace it when the working policy changes. This changes task-local behavior,
not platform permissions. Facts and concrete plans may remain scoped Memory or
task_prompt contributions; they do not become system directives automatically.
Update canonical content alongside changed summaries/projections. Declare exact
depends_on_refs for validity and supersedes_refs for replaced guidance; review
notices suspend stale guidance until revalidated. Historical basis_refs alone do
not imply a validity dependency.

Admission and trial contract (also enforced on research adoption):
- Memory requires explicit validity={scope:task|episode|level|state}; runtime binds
  omitted task_ref/instance_ref to the current instance. A control rule may be task
  scoped only under its stated control-mode conditions; a map is level scoped.
  Budget, current position and the latest frame are already runtime state. Do not
  store duplicate permanent versions. Unscoped historical memory is review-only.
- Every new skill requires a creator-authored use-trigger description and
  executable instructions (or content), the complete procedural body the parent
  will load. A single-situation, untested procedure is a valid pool candidate;
  creation does not certify utility. Structured method metadata is optional. If
  supplied, preserve its authored fields exactly and use arrays of strings for
  list fields; runtime rejects malformed metadata but does not invent a dossier.
- Structure and creator provenance are admission checks, NOT semantic validation.
  Design a contrast or challenge when evidence, access and budget permit. A clearly
  labelled unvalidated candidate may be selected for its first bounded trial; do
  not claim the challenge ran when it did not. After use assess the exact decision with actual-use
  evidence. Exposure-only assessments cannot support utility. Unsupported results
  require revising, suspending or retiring the resource; do not silently reuse it.
- Auto-Research targets a sustained question about a method, representation,
  search/planning strategy or uncertainty. Compare alternatives and evaluate on
  evidence separate from construction; a recap alone is not research progress.
  A research-only negative finding is valid. One-off independent reasoning belongs
  in delegate_task; create a reusable role only when a context recipe is useful.
- System-prompt segments carry justified continuing behavioral guidance; Skills
  carry procedures including discovery, planning, strategy comparison and recovery.
  A small or first-use computation may warrant a Pi Tool for precision, consistency
  or saved reasoning; inspect the available substrate rather than assuming only
  expensive calculations merit tools. A clean/preconfigured Subagent context may
  help on the same evidence, even once. Memory carries scoped facts and hypotheses.
  A system policy need only persist across its declared applicable decisions, not
  the whole game. Relative balance means fitting responsibilities, never equal
  resource counts, mandatory permanence or a creation quota.

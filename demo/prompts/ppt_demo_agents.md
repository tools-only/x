# Optional task guidance

Use this as a source of ideas, not a required procedure or report format. You may change your approach before a problem repeats when evidence suggests an adjustment could help; you may also decide that direct task work is the better use of time.

When considering an adjustment, ask yourself what part of the work it could improve, what result would support keeping it, and whether enough work remains to recover its cost. Prefer a small, reversible trial when it can answer that question. Use it in real subsequent work, then keep, revise, or retire it based on what happened.

Harness changes may include prompts, skills, tools, subagents, memory, and task-local ways of organizing or running work. Reuse existing capabilities when suitable. Component counts, calls, and check results describe activity; they do not establish benefit. Consider the task outcome, effort saved or added, elapsed time, and relevant child work together.

Harness components can only be changed with `reflect_component(op="define"|"redefine"|"restore")`; generic workspace tools are for presentation work and reject component-storage paths.

The five closure targets and their evidence conditions are:

1. `memory`: a stable-in-scope fact or task state that later decisions need to retrieve, supported by exact real observation/action references. Transient frame details must retain their scope and reconsider condition.
2. `skills`: an executable observation, planning, decision or recovery procedure, including a provisional single-situation method. Identify a real applicable use; repeated or cross-level use is not required for admission.
3. `tools`: a deterministic computation or authorized adapter operation fully expressible by the parent-visible task-tool contract. Small first-use checks are valid. The parent inspects the substrate and authors the implementation; the child cannot return a tool body.
4. `subagents`: a persistent independent-analysis role justified by a real need for competing interpretations, verification, or open-ended analysis. After routing, delegate one concrete read-only ARC question to the created role.
5. `system_prompt`: a parent-authored scoped working policy, including provisional exploration or planning guidance for a task or level. Use `component=system_prompt` with content and declared scope, then explicitly assemble its exact version; no creator is required. If using the compatibility projection route instead, supply `prompt_channel=system_prompt` and its provenance-bound `system_prompt_basis`. A task-wide verified invariant is not a prerequisite. Preserve uncertainty and keep instance facts out of the behavioral policy.

Do not invent evidence or ask the child to create a component merely to satisfy coverage. An inconclusive report is valid research but does not close that component. Continue evidence collection and use a later focused run when its stated condition becomes supportable. Provider output-length boundaries are handled inside the Auto-Research runtime; do not restart the question or duplicate completed approvals.

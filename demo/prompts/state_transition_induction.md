# Learn mechanisms from interaction

Treat experience as before-state, executed action, after-state transitions.
Establish what changed and what stayed invariant before interpreting why. Keep
raw observation, derived measurement and hypothesis distinct. Temporal succession
alone is not causation: elapsed time, autonomous dynamics, hidden conditions and
observation noise can explain differences. Missing state is unknown, not unchanged.

Compare different actions in comparable states and the same action under different
conditions. Infer effects, affected entities or variables, prerequisites and limits
without assuming action meanings or familiar task mechanics. Preserve competing
explanations. Prefer a permitted, affordable interaction whose predicted outcomes
distinguish those explanations; do not repeat observations that cannot discriminate.
When controlled comparisons are unavailable, state the limitation rather than
calling an association a validated mechanism. Do not assume reset or replay exists.

Progress from local testable regularities to conditional mechanisms, then compose
them into useful behavior and strategies. Higher-level candidates must identify
their supporting rules and unresolved dependencies. This is increasing explanatory
and behavioral complexity, not a mandatory component ladder or exhaustive action
survey. Goal hypotheses and useful action can proceed while uncertainties remain.
Counterexamples require revising scope, rejecting a rule or revisiting dependencies.

At each incremental extraction answer: which transitions now have predictive
explanations, which uncertainty most limits the next decision, and which repeated
work can become a reusable capability? Record a distinguishing next experiment,
predictions for alternatives and a falsifier, or explain why no experiment is needed
or possible. Reference actual window evidence; retrieve exact records before making
claims that excerpts cannot support.

Use periodic windows as evidence entry points. A research problem may require
nonadjacent successes, failures or whole-stage outcomes within granted access.
Before delegation connect concrete starting material to an attainable intermediate
objective and a reachable local evaluation: expected observable state/output,
success/failure criterion, available executor and cost/stop bound. Gather missing
prerequisites or narrow the question rather than requesting an unsupported global
solution. Do not mistake a defined evaluation for a guaranteed decisive outcome.

Investigate how the agent reached or failed to reach a conclusion as well as what
the environment did. Construct candidate methods/representations using relevant
prior knowledge, distinguish these candidates from verified mechanisms, and test
them locally. Preserve the problem, competing explanations and pending test across
phases in existing research state; use later method applications as new evidence.

At consolidation, test whether local rules compose, inspect contradictions and
unsupported dependencies, and check actual use of earlier capabilities. Repeated
calculation suggests a tool; reusable observation/testing/judgment suggests a skill;
continuing independent reasoning suggests a role; conditional decision guidance
suggests task-prompt projection. Facts belong in memory. One pattern can support
complementary capabilities. Prefer reuse or revision over duplication, and specify
next use and semantic validation. Saving more facts alone does not finish a method
extraction goal. No supported pattern is a valid outcome with an explicit reason.

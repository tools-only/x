This is a real ARC acceptance run for one narrow lifecycle:

cross-situation induction -> method candidate -> parent adoption -> actual use -> effect assessment -> feedback research.

The environment is one irreversible online life. You are the only actor allowed to call `arc_action`; Auto-Research children remain read-only and may only return bounded experiment requests for you to consider. Do not RESET merely to manufacture another context, and do not describe different situations in the same level as different tasks or episodes.

First collect enough real transitions to compare at least two materially different situations. A situation difference must be grounded in observable conditions such as position, destination class, current hypothesis, outcome signature, or an interaction effect. Prefer contrasts such as a successful move versus a blocked move, or an ordinary move versus entering a special tile. At the pending 5-action review, cite exact observations from the different situations and construct a parameterized method candidate whose applicability, limits, next use, and observable validation are explicit. A trajectory summary or a location-specific fact is insufficient.

Start the returned Auto-Research handoff in blocking mode unless the current ARC state makes one immediate action strictly necessary to preserve a time-sensitive opportunity. The research goal is to challenge, refine, or locally evaluate the method across the cited situations. It may use the stored trajectory and granted resources, but cannot act. If new interaction evidence is required, the child must return a structured request and you decide whether to execute it within the remaining action budget.

When the child completes, inspect the result. Adopt it only if its method delivery is supported and useful; adoption remains your decision. If you adopt it, use `task_harness(action='adopt_research', research_run_ref=...)`. On a later real ARC action, actually apply the exact adopted method version and include its exact resource ref in `arc_action.decision.basis_refs`. Exposure or agreement alone does not count as use.

After observing that action, call `task_harness(action='assess_effect')` with the real action observation reference, a supported/unsupported/inconclusive verdict, the consequence, and remaining uncertainty. This assessment must describe method behavior under the observed situation, not claim whole-game improvement. Confirm that the runtime creates a feedback handoff on the same research line, then start that feedback research in blocking mode. The feedback child must compare the prediction with the actual outcome and revise, bound, or reject the method; it must not merely restate the latest action.

Continue normal play only as needed to complete this lifecycle or until WIN/action exhaustion. If a child result has no supportable method delivery, preserve that negative result and use the next 5-action window to form a better cross-situation candidate. Do not create a component merely to satisfy this probe, do not manually copy a child proposal into a harness component, and do not count a parent-direct resource as Auto-Research adoption.

# Research focus: composition

Investigate whether the selected capabilities work together for the proposed
purpose. Identify their interfaces, dependency versions, ordering assumptions,
and interactions. Compare end-to-end observations with what each component alone
would predict. Look for incompatibilities and failures at handoff boundaries.

Report which combinations and conditions the evidence supports, which dependency
or interaction remains uncertain, and how to test it. Working components do not
by themselves establish a working composition.

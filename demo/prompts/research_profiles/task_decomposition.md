# Research focus: task decomposition

Clarify the task objective and identify subproblems whose results would advance
it. Examine dependencies, unknown prerequisites, and alternative decompositions.
Separate what can be solved independently from what requires another result or
new evidence. Check whether the proposed decomposition covers the task.

Report a justified decomposition, important unresolved dependencies, and useful
next investigations. A proposed plan is not evidence that its steps have succeeded.

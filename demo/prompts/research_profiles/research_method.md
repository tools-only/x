# Research focus: research method

Investigate whether the research approach can answer its question. Examine
question formulation, evidence selection, measurement, experiment design, and
evaluation. Check for confounding, circular validation, missing counterexamples,
and claims stronger than the observations.

Report weaknesses supported by the record, useful methodological revisions, and
how their effect could be evaluated. Preserve negative or inconclusive results;
agreement between investigators is not independent validation.

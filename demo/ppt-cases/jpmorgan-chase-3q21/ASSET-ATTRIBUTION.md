## Earnings release text

`assets/material.txt` contains the earnings-release text corresponding to JPMorgan Chase's Q3 2021 earnings. The source is SEC EDGAR Exhibit 99.1: <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/19617/000001961721000446/a3q21erfexhibit991narrative.htm>. The SEC's reuse statement for EDGAR filing content is at <https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions>.

The original PDF and its rendered page images are not included. `material.txt` is the source representation used by the task agent.
